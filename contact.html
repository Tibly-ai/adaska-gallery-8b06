What we can help with

Four ways people write to us

01

Acquisitions

Ask for the full condition report, verso photographs and framing options on any piece in the collection. We hold a work for 48 hours while you decide, and payment can be split across three months on anything above $2,000.

Browse the collection

02

Commissions

Eleven of our thirty-one artists take commissions. Send dimensions, a photo of the room and any palette you are working around, and we will suggest two or three artists whose hand fits. Studio time usually runs eight to fourteen weeks.

Meet the artists

03

Private viewings

The Hudson Street room holds about a dozen works at a time and we hang it around your visit. Sessions run 45 minutes. If you cannot make it to Tribeca, we do the same walkthrough on a video call with the works on the wall.

Wed to Sat, 11am to 6pm. Mon and Tue by appointment.

04

Shipping and framing

Everything leaves here framed behind museum glass, crated and insured for its full value. Domestic delivery takes five to seven working days; international is quoted per crate. Ask us about customs paperwork before you order and we will have it ready.

Third floor, Tribeca

A quiet room and a slow conversation

Adaska Gallery has worked from the same loft since 2011. Ring the buzzer marked Verso and take the freight lift to three. There is a bench, decent coffee, and nobody hovering while you look.

Most first-time collectors leave with a work on paper. That is a good place to start, and we are happy to talk you through it.
