Work of the month

01

Long Room, Late Light

Ilse Marchetti, 2023

Marchetti paints the same north-facing room over and over, once a season. This is the winter version: oil laid thin over a warm ground, then scraped back until the linen shows through along the top edge. It reads quiet from across a room and busy up close.

About the gallery

A small room on the third floor, since 2011

Verso started as one wall in a shared studio on Hudson Street. We kept the wall and took the rest of the floor. The programme is deliberately narrow: painting, drawing, and print by artists we can visit in person, most of them within a day's train ride.

Every work is photographed here, measured twice, and condition-checked before it is listed. If you are buying from a screen we would rather over-explain than surprise you, so ask for extra photographs, a wall mock-up, or a video in daylight. We send them the same day.
