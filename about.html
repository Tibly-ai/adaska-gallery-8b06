About Est. 2011 · New York

We show fewer works,
and we live with them
far longer.

Verso began in a third-floor loft on Hudson Street with eleven paintings and a borrowed ladder. The programme has grown to thirty-one living artists, though the method has not changed: we hang one wall at a time, sit with the work for a week, and only then write about it.

Everything is photographed in daylight at actual scale, so what you see on screen is close to what arrives in the crate.

What we look for

Work made by hand, at a scale someone can actually live with, by an artist we can still call in ten years.

In practice that means oil and egg tempera, drawings in graphite and ink, monotypes, and editions capped at thirty. We avoid speculation: no flipping, no waitlists sold to the highest bidder, and no work priced on hype. Artists set their prices with us once a year, and we hold them.

How we work
with collectors

Most of our sales begin with a question in an email and end with a crate on a Wednesday. Between those two points, five things happen. You can use all of them or one.

  • 01

    Private viewings

    We pull up to six works, hang them on a clean wall, and leave you alone with them for as long as you want. Wednesday through Saturday in the loft, or by appointment early in the week. Coffee is real; the lighting is 3000K, same as most apartments.

  • 02

    Home trial

    Within Manhattan and Brooklyn we deliver a work on approval and hang it ourselves. Keep it on the wall for seven days. If it stays, we invoice. If it goes, we collect it and there is no charge.

  • 03

    Framing and conservation

    Our framer works two blocks away in maple, walnut and painted poplar, with museum glass on anything on paper. Rag mats, reversible hinges, no adhesive tape. Older works get condition-checked before they leave.

  • 04

    Shipping and installation

    Everything ships framed, insured for the full purchase price, in a custom crate or a double-wall carton depending on size. Domestic delivery is included. For anything over four feet we send an installer with a laser level and a stud finder.

  • 05

    Building a collection

    For collectors buying a fourth or fortieth work, we keep a running record of what you own, what you passed on, and why. Advisory on secondary-market pieces, appraisals for insurance, and honest talk about what is worth waiting for.
